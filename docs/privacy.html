Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Euterpe is an open-source Android musical idea generator developed by Tasos Kleisas. This policy describes the information handled by the Euterpe application and this website.

Summary: Euterpe operates 100% offline on your device. It does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs, and does not request internet, microphone, camera, location, or contacts permissions.

No account or personal data

Euterpe does not require sign-in or any personal information. No third-party analytics, advertising, or telemetry libraries are included in the app.

Audio and local data

All audio synthesis, percussion, Lua idea generation, and multi-layer loop recordings are processed locally in real time on your device. Recorded layers remain strictly in memory and are never uploaded. Bundled Lua generator scripts are extracted to app-private storage so the local audio engine can read them.

Permissions

Euterpe does not request sensitive Android permissions (no internet, microphone, camera, location, contacts, or external storage access).

Children's privacy

Euterpe is a general-purpose musical tool containing no ads, purchases, or social features, and does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated policy will be published on this page with a new update date.

Contact

Questions or bug reports can be sent to tasos.kleisas@gmail.com or opened at github.com/tkleisas/euterpe/issues.
